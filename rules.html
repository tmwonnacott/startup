In NeverWord your goal is to never spell a word, but don't spell a never-word.
A never-word is a group of letters that can never become a word, like "Qezx." You and your opponent take turns adding letters until
1) Someone spells a real word and loses
OR
2) Someone calls bluff. When someone calls bluff, the opponent has to say what word can be spelled starting with the current letters, if they can't think of anything, they lose.
